Priority Splitters 1.0.1 for Moon Sweepers
==========================================

Gives every splitter a priority output and every merger a priority input.
Look at a splitter or merger from within 6 m and press P to cycle it through
left first, right first and round-robin (vanilla). The S or M cone on the
machine leans toward the priority lane. Choices are saved per save slot in
BepInEx\config\PrioritySplitters\slot-N.json.

Requirements
  - BepInEx 6.0.0-be.791 (Unity.IL2CPP win-x64)
  - Moon Sweepers BepInEx Patcher (https://www.nexusmods.com/moonsweepers/mods/9)

Install
  Extract this archive into the game folder (next to MoonSweepers.exe) so the
  DLL ends up at BepInEx\plugins\PrioritySplitters\PrioritySplitters.dll.

Uninstall
  Delete BepInEx\plugins\PrioritySplitters. Saves are not modified.

Settings: BepInEx\config\danbopes.moonsweepers.prioritysplitters.cfg
Source:   https://github.com/danbopes/MoonSweepers-PrioritySplitters
